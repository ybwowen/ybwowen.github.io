Bowen Yang (杨博文)

I am a junior undergraduate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Paris Elite Institute of Technology, majoring in Information Engineering and French. At RethinkLab, I work with Prof. Junchi Yan and Prof. Xiaosong Jia on robotics and vision–language–action (VLA) models, focusing on robust policy learning, representation alignment, and mechanism interpretability.

I am set to graduate in June 2027 and am actively seeking research internship opportunities in robotics, embodied AI, and robot learning. I study how multimodal representations affect perception, action generation, and generalization, with particular interests in object grounding, temporal skill recognition, and geometric perception.
